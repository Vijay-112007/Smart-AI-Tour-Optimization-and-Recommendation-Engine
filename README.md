# Smart-AI-Tour-Optimization-and-Recommendation-Engine

This is an AI project which is to develop a system that helps a traveler to plan his journey or tour and optimize the plan in real time

## Problem Statement:

People travels to different places using different transportations. While they book the tickets for any places, they often face difficulty to plan the trip from the starting point to ending point i.e. from one accommodation to another accommodation for simple. Like often face challenges while looking for cabs and availability of transportation to reach the checkpoints in their entire journey.

## Proposed Solution:

The proposed AI system identifies the bookings of a user and plan the entire trip either normal trip or tour from starting point to the ending point with necessary recommendation and optimization of trip checkpoints and adapts to the current location and journey timings and recommend the best possible way to make the journey smooth i.e. like reminding the sleeping hours, availability of transportation, adapting to different conditions in the journey etc. and notifies the user about the journey details always in real time.

## For your reference :

Abstract— Planning and managing a trip requires travelers to consider multiple factors, including destination preferences, available time, transportation, budget, accommodation, attraction schedules, and unexpected changes during the journey. Existing itinerary recommendation approaches primarily focus on generating optimized travel plans based on predefined user preferences and constraints. However, such plans may become inefficient when real-world conditions change after the trip has begun. This project proposes a Smart AI Trip Recommendation and Optimization Engine that integrates personalized trip planning with context-aware, real-time itinerary optimization. The system provides two modes of operation: Trip Planning, where users describe their travel requirements and preferences, and the system analyzes these requirements to recommend suitable destinations, attractions, transportation, accommodation, and an optimized itinerary; and Real-Time Trip Assistance, where the system continuously evaluates the user's current location, time, planned activities, transportation conditions, and other contextual information to recommend the most suitable next actions and dynamically adjust the itinerary. The proposed system aims to optimize travel time, cost, convenience, and user satisfaction while maintaining important temporal and logistical constraints. By combining intelligent recommendation, route and itinerary optimization, and dynamic replanning, the system seeks to provide a more adaptive and personalized travel experience than conventional static itinerary planning approaches.
